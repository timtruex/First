## Who we are (the story every post can lean on)

A couple from Bellville, Ohio who live in a restored 1850 brick home, rent out an
1875 one-room schoolhouse they converted into a two-bedroom home, love to
travel, and are chasing the best historic places in America to spend a night.
Grandparents of six. Not influencers — restorers who travel.

## Voice

- Warm, curious, plain-spoken. Midwestern, not slick.
- Lead with the human story of the building (who built it, who used it, what
  happened to it, who saved it), then the stay.
- Specific numbers and dates beat adjectives. "Forty farm kids, one woodstove"
  beats "charming and cozy."
- Never invent history. If unsure, say "local accounts say…" and cite it.
- No hype words: *stunning, breathtaking, hidden gem, bucket list, must-see*.

## Content pillars

1. **Sleep in History** — one featured stay, its story, how to book.
2. **Then & Now** — what the building was vs. what it is.
3. **Old House Lessons** — what we learned restoring 1850/1875 buildings.
4. **On the Road** — our own trips to historic stays.
5. **Roundups** — "5 churches you can sleep in," by type or state.

## Hard rules

1. Never post a photo or video without written permission from its owner. Log the
   permission (who, date, scope) in `data/stays.csv` → `permission` column.
2. No AI-generated or AI-altered imagery presented as a real place. Basic
   edits (crop, exposure, color) are fine.
3. Every historical claim in a draft carries a source link. Unsourced claims are
   cut or softened before approval.
4. Grandchildren: no faces, names, teams, or schools without their parents' OK.
5. Paid features are disclosed with Instagram's paid-partnership label and/or
   `#ad` (FTC endorsement rules).
6. No automated DMs, follows, likes, or comments (platform terms). Agents draft;
   Tim sends.
7. Nothing publishes without Tim's approval.
