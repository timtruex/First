# Outreach templates

Agents draft; **Tim sends** from the brand email (e.g. hello@oldwallsnewmemories.com).
Personalize the first line every time — the Historian notes give you something real.

## 1. Feature request (free, early stage)

**Subject:** Featuring your [schoolhouse/church/depot] on Old Walls, New Memories?

Hi [first name],

[One specific, true line about their building — e.g. "I didn't know an 1871
depot could sleep five people. I love that you kept the ticket window."]

We restored an 1875 one-room schoolhouse in Bellville, Ohio that we
rent out, and we live in an 1850 brick home. We've started Old Walls, New
Memories, an Instagram/TikTok page about historic places people can actually
stay in.

We'd love to feature [property name] in a short video. Could we use [3–8] of
your listing photos, credited to you with a link to book directly? There's no
cost; you'd see the draft before anything posts if you'd like.

If yes, just reply "yes" — that's all the permission we need. And if you know
the building's history (who built it, what it was used for), we'd love to hear
it.

Thanks,
Tim
Old Walls, New Memories · Bellville, Ohio

## 2. Follow-up (7 days, once only)

Hi [first name] — just floating this back up. Happy to send the draft first.
If now's not a good time, no worries at all. — Tim

## 3. Thank-you + post live

Hi [first name] — your feature is live: [link]. Thank you! Feel free to share
or repost it. — Tim

## 4. Paid feature offer (only after ~5K followers — GM will tell you when)

Saved for later. Pricing and the media kit will be drafted once we have real

## Permission logging

When a host replies yes, record in `data/stays.csv`:
`permission` = `yes YYYY-MM-DD photos:<scope> by:<name>` and save the reply text
in `outreach/permissions/<id>.txt`.
