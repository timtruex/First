## Slides
1. **Cover:** "5 lighthouses you can actually sleep in"
2. **Borden Flats Light, MA** — You sleep inside the tower. No Wi-Fi, no running water. Sells out every year.
3. **Nauset Beach Light, MA** — Rent the 1874 keeper's house on Cape Cod.
4. **Little River Light, ME** — An island light. Bring your own food, water and sheets.
5. **Cove Point Lighthouse, MD** — The keepers' duplex is now two cottages on the Chesapeake.
6. **DeTour Reef Light, MI** — Don't just stay. Be the keeper, out in Lake Huron.
7. **CTA:** "Which one could you handle? Save this for your next trip."

## Caption
Five lighthouses where you can spend the night — from a Cape Cod keeper's house
to a tower with no running water. Which one could you do? 🔦
