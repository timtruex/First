## Voiceover
> In the 1890s, Rudyard Kipling built this house in the hills of Vermont and
> named it Naulakha. Here he wrote The Jungle Book. He left in 1896 and never
> came back. Today a nonprofit, Landmark Trust USA, rents it out — four
> bedrooms, sleeps eight — and uses the money to keep it standing. You can sit
> at the desk where Mowgli was born.
