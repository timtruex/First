## Hook

## Voiceover (each line paired with a shot inside our schoolhouse)
> One room. One teacher, often a teenager. Kids from six to sixteen together.
> The older kids carried in the firewood and water. You read out of a McGuffey
> Reader, written by an Ohio teacher. You wrote on a slate. The school year
> was planned around planting and harvest. Bad behavior? You might end up
> standing in the corner. This room in Bellville, Ohio was built for exactly
> that. Tonight, someone's sleeping in it.

## Shot list
Woodstove or where it stood · the tall windows · a slate (prop) · a McGuffey

## Caption
One room, one teacher, every age together. This is what school looked like when
our schoolhouse was built in 1875 — and now it's a place to stay.
