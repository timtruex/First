## Hook (first 2 seconds)
On-screen text: **"We live in an 1850 house. We rent out an 1875 schoolhouse."**

## Script (Tim, to camera, walking toward the front door of the house)
> We live in a brick house built in 1850. A few years ago we bought a one-room
> schoolhouse from 1875 and turned it into a two-bedroom home people can stay in.
> Somewhere along the way we got hooked — schoolhouses, churches, train depots,
> lighthouses. If it's old and you can sleep in it, we want to find it.
> This is Old Walls, New Memories. Follow along — we're bringing you the best
> places in America to spend a night in history.

## Shot list
1. Wide: Tim (and spouse, if willing) walking up to the 1850 house (5s)
2. Close-up: date stone / old brick / original door hardware (3s)
3. Exterior of the schoolhouse, slow push-in (5s)
4. Interior of the schoolhouse — the most "schoolhouse" detail (bell, windows, ceiling) (5s)
5. Tim to camera, last line (10s)

## Caption
We live in an 1850 brick house in Bellville, Ohio, and we restored an 1875
one-room schoolhouse into a home you can stay in. Now we're on a mission: find
the best historic places in America you can actually spend the night in.
Schoolhouses, churches, depots, lighthouses — tell us which one we should
visit first 👇
