amzvid Capture

One click on an Amazon listing copies the title, bullets, price and photo URLs. Paste it into your bot and you are done typing.

amzvid Capture

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar. Clicking it here does nothing.

Setup

  1. Show your bookmarks bar if it is hidden: ⇧⌘B in Chrome, View → Show Favourites Bar in Safari.
  2. Drag the yellow button onto the bar.

Using it

  1. Open the Amazon listing and scroll the photo gallery once so the images load. The capture reads what the page has loaded.
  2. Click amzvid Capture in your bookmarks bar. A green banner confirms what it copied.
  3. Paste into your Telegram bot, or run amzvid paste --go in a terminal.
Why a bookmarklet and not a scraper. This runs in your browser, on a page you are already looking at, when you click it. There is no automated request to Amazon, so there is nothing to rate-limit or block, and it stays clear of the Associates agreement's bar on automated access. It is a structured copy-paste.
